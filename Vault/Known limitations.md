# Known Limitations

Stated directly in the app's own README — worth keeping visible here, not softened.

> [!warning] Not a validated instrument
> This is a conversation tool, not a validated psychometric instrument. Self-report captures how people *describe* themselves, which isn't always how they behave. Treat the score as a conversation starter, not a prediction.

- **Weights are editorial judgment.** Category percentages reflect organized opinion, not measurement.
- **Nothing has been calibrated against real outcomes.** Validating the scoring needs roughly 300 opt-in profiles paired with a relationship-satisfaction measure, then re-deriving weights by regression — and honestly checking whether the Alignment Index correlates with satisfaction at all. See "Part 3 — Making the numbers real" in the project's v3 build spec for the plan already on file.
- **Actual personality similarity doesn't predict satisfaction** in established couples, per the research the project cites — this is why the tool frames itself as a *difference map*, not a predictor.
- Trademarked instruments (MBTI, DiSC) are deliberately not reproduced or claimed — see [[Decisions Log]].
